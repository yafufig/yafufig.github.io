Investigation

I traced future-data leakage to the interaction between timeline preprocessing and positional embeddings. After fixing the pipeline, the apparent uplift disappeared. I proposed timeline and embedding checks for the pre-training protocol.

What I contributed

Model reproduction and historical-data comparisons. I also segmented active and passive users through activity CDFs, analysed recall@50 by segment, and built dashboards for cohort-based quality monitoring.

Careful offline evaluation and willingness to question an apparently positive result. HSTU and PLE are existing research methods I implemented, not publications I authored.

An offline evaluation checklist for a support agent

Written for this portfolio as a general example, informed by my evaluation experience.

  1. Freeze a labelled dialogue set and record its version. Split by conversation, keeping future information out of the input.
  2. Define intent accuracy, answer completeness and escalation criteria before comparing versions.
  3. Run the same examples against both versions. Keep prompt, model and retrieval configuration with each result.
  4. Review failure categories: unsupported claims, incorrect routing, missing knowledge and failed escalation.
  5. Inspect segment-level results and low-quality cases. Record limitations and unresolved regressions before release.

An offline gain is a reason to investigate further. It does not by itself establish a production improvement.
